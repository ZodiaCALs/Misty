# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve Misty. This page explains how to propose a change and how your contribution is licensed.

## Propose a change

- Open an issue that describes the problem and names the requirement identifiers it touches.
- For a token change, edit `tokens/misty.tokens.json`, run the build and the checks described in the README, and include their output.
- Follow the versioning rules in clause 3.6 of the standard. A new MUST that existing products would fail waits for the next major version.

## How your contribution is licensed

- **Code, tokens and icons**, everything under Apache-2.0: you contribute under the Apache License 2.0, as its section 5 describes.
- **Text and figures of the standard, and the documents**, everything under CC BY-ND 4.0: you license your contribution to ZodiaCALs under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, so that ZodiaCALs can edit it and publish it as part of the standard under CC BY-ND 4.0.
- Sign off each commit with `git commit -s` to confirm the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 (<https://developercertificate.org>): that you wrote the contribution or have the right to submit it.

## Translations

Unofficial translations don’t need a pull request; the translation permission in `LICENSE.md` covers them. To propose an official translation, open an issue first.
